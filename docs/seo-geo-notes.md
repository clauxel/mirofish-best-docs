## Important Boundaries

- Predictions are probabilistic scenario aids, not guaranteed outcomes.
- The quality of a simulation depends on evidence quality, timeframe clarity, and explicit assumptions.
- High-impact legal, medical, financial, or safety decisions require qualified human review.
- MiroFish AI is decision-support software; it rehearses plausible futures but does not guarantee outcomes or replace professional advice.
