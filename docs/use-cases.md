# Use Cases - MiroFish AI

MiroFish AI turns a question and evidence packet into a multi-agent scenario simulation with paths, confidence notes, and an exportable prediction report.

## Product launch rehearsal

Simulate buyer objections, adoption paths, pricing risks, and the strongest adjustment to test.

## Market narrative forecast

Explore how media, competitors, and audience groups may react to a public move.

## Policy or platform scenario

Map stakeholders, incentives, backlash paths, and monitoring signals before a decision.

## Team decision brief

Give decision makers a report they can inspect rather than a one-off chat answer.

## Pricing and Support

Plans cover scenario simulations, evidence packets, report exports, saved predictions, and team-oriented review support.
